# [[Lyra Dawnbringer]] — Budget Angels & Lifegain (Bracket 2)

**★★★ (3/5) — a solid Bracket 2 deck** · **Bracket 2 (Core)** · 0 Game Changers · ~€55

## TL;DR

Mono-white Angel tribal on a budget. [[Lyra Dawnbringer]] gives every other Angel +1/+1 and lifelink, which
turns each combat step into a big lifegain turn. The deck spends that life on more 4/4 Angels, extra cards,
and +1/+1 counters, then flies over the table. There are no combos and no Game Changers. It wins the honest
way: by attacking in the air.

## How the deck wins

1. **Build a flock.** Cheap Angels and Angel cost reducers ([[Giada, Font of Hope]], [[Starnheim Aspirant]],
   [[Herald's Horn]]) get several flyers out before Lyra arrives on turn 4 or 5.
2. **Turn on lifelink.** With Lyra out, every Angel has lifelink. Lifegain triggers stack up on top of that:
   [[Bishop of Wings]], [[Righteous Valkyrie]], [[Angel of Vitality]] and [[Dazzling Angel]] all gain life
   whenever an Angel enters.
3. **Turn life into Angels and cards.** [[Resplendent Angel]], [[Valkyrie Harbinger]] and [[Angelic Accord]]
   make a 4/4 Angel every turn you gain enough life. [[Well of Lost Dreams]], [[Dawn of Hope]],
   [[Exemplar of Light]] and [[Sigarda's Splendor]] turn life into cards.
4. **Air superiority.** [[Righteous Valkyrie]] (+2/+2 to everything once you're 7 life above your starting
   total), [[Patchwork Banner]], [[Angelic Field Marshal]] and [[Lyra, Archangel of Dawn]] counters make the
   flyers lethal. [[Serra's Emissary]] (choose "creature") and [[Sephara, Sky's Blade]] (indestructible
   flyers) keep the attack going through blockers and removal.

## Card roles & tags

**Engine (numbered, in the order the plan runs):**
- `1) Angel Flock`: the Angel bodies and the cards that make them cheaper.
- `2) Lifegain Triggers`: cards that gain extra life on top of lifelink.
- `3) Life Into Angels`: payoffs that turn life gained into tokens, cards or counters.
- `4) Air Superiority`: anthems and finishers that close the game.

**Support pillars:** `A) Mana Advantage`, `B) Card Advantage`, `C) Interaction`, `D) Resilience`, `E) Lands`.

### 1) Angel Flock
- [[Giada, Font of Hope]]: a 2-drop that taps for Angel mana. Every later Angel enters bigger.
- [[Youthful Valkyrie]]: a 2-drop flyer that grows with every Angel you play.
- [[Starnheim Aspirant]]: Angels cost {2} less. The best mana card in the deck.
- [[Herald of War]]: grows every attack and makes Angels cheaper with each counter.
- [[Herald's Horn]]: a cost reducer that also hands you an Angel from the top of the deck most turns.
- [[Wojek Investigator]]: a cheap flyer that makes Clues while you're behind on cards.
- [[Inspiring Overseer]]: gain 1, draw 1, on a flyer. Pure value.
- [[Firemane Commando]]: draws a card every time you attack with two or more. That's every turn in this deck.
- [[Merchant of Truth]]: a Clue whenever your nontoken creatures die, which softens board wipes.
- [[Baneslayer Angel]]: a lifelink, first-strike threat that doesn't need Lyra.
- [[Angel of Finality]]: exiles a graveyard, a cheap answer to reanimator decks.
- [[Angel of Sanctions]]: an Angel that exiles any nonland permanent, then comes back later with embalm.
- [[Sunblast Angel]]: kills every tapped creature, a one-sided wipe after opponents attack.
- [[Angel of Serenity]]: exiles three creatures. The late-game reset.
- [[Emeria Shepherd]]: brings back permanents with every land drop, straight onto the battlefield off Plains.
- [[Herald of Eternal Dawn]]: flash it in and you can't lose the game. Your safety net.
- [[Shattered Angel]]: gains 3 every time an opponent plays a land. A slow, steady life source.

### 2) Lifegain Triggers
- [[Bishop of Wings]]: gain 4 per Angel that enters, and a 1/1 flyer whenever an Angel dies.
- [[Righteous Valkyrie]]: gains life equal to each new Angel's toughness. At +7 life, your team gets +2/+2.
- [[Angel of Vitality]]: +1 to every lifegain event, and a 4/5 flyer at 25 or more life.
- [[Dazzling Angel]]: 1 life for every creature you play, which feeds the token payoffs.
- [[Scroll of Avacyn]]: a 1-mana cantrip that gains 5 if you have an Angel. That's enough for [[Resplendent Angel]].
- [[Fumigate]]: a board wipe that gains you a big chunk of life.
- [[Seraph Sanctuary]]: a land that gains life whenever an Angel enters.

### 3) Life Into Angels
- [[Resplendent Angel]]: gain 5 in a turn and get a 4/4 Angel at end step. One Lyra-buffed attack does it.
- [[Valkyrie Harbinger]]: the same deal with a lower bar (4 life), plus lifelink of its own.
- [[Angelic Accord]]: the enchantment version: 4 life in a turn, 4/4 flyer.
- [[Divine Visitation]]: every token becomes a 4/4 Angel, including [[Bishop of Wings]] spirits and [[Court of Grace]] spirits.
- [[Court of Grace]]: monarch card draw plus a 4/4 Angel every upkeep while you hold the crown.
- [[Lyra, Archangel of Dawn]]: every lifegain event puts +1/+1 counters on all your Angels.
- [[Exemplar of Light]]: grows and draws whenever you gain life.
- [[Vanguard Seraph]]: surveils on your first lifegain each turn.
- [[Well of Lost Dreams]]: turns spare mana and life into cards. Your late-game refuel.
- [[Dawn of Hope]]: {2} per lifegain trigger for a card. It also makes lifelink soldiers.
- [[Sigarda's Splendor]]: a free card every upkeep while your life keeps climbing.
- [[Cradle of Vitality]]: turns a big lifelink hit into that many +1/+1 counters.

### 4) Air Superiority
- [[Angelic Field Marshal]]: a 4/4 flyer while Lyra is out, and your team attacks without tapping (vigilance).
- [[Patchwork Banner]]: Angels +1/+1, and it taps for mana.
- [[Sephara, Sky's Blade]]: often cast for {W} by tapping four flyers. Your flyers become indestructible.
- [[Serra's Emissary]]: name "creature" and you and your team can't be blocked or hurt by creatures.

### A) Mana Advantage
- [[Sol Ring]], [[Arcane Signet]], [[Mind Stone]], [[Fellwar Stone]], [[Marble Diamond]],
  [[Worn Powerstone]] and [[Commander's Sphere]]: rocks that get Lyra out on turn 4.
- [[Wayfarer's Bauble]] and [[Archaeomancer's Map]]: Plains to keep up with green ramp decks.

### C) Interaction
- [[Swords to Plowshares]], [[Path to Exile]]: 1-mana creature exile.
- [[Generous Gift]], [[Stroke of Midnight]], [[Oblivion Ring]], [[Banishing Light]]: answers to any permanent.
- [[Valorous Stance]]: kills a big creature or saves Lyra.
- [[Wrath of God]], [[Austere Command]]: resets for when you're behind. [[Austere Command]] can spare your own Angels (MV 4+ is a separate mode).

### D) Resilience
- [[Unbreakable Formation]], [[Make a Stand]]: team indestructible against a board wipe, or to win combat.
- [[Swiftfoot Boots]]: hexproof and haste for Lyra.
- [[Kindred Boon]]: makes key Angels indestructible over time.

### E) Lands
- 31 [[Plains]] plus [[Seraph Sanctuary]], [[Path of Ancestry]], [[Temple of the False God]],
  [[Myriad Landscape]], [[Secluded Steppe]], [[Castle Ardenvale]] (makes 1/1 tokens, which become Angels with
  [[Divine Visitation]]) and [[Rogue's Passage]] (makes a creature unblockable).

## Play guide

**Mulligan:** keep hands with 3–4 lands plus either a mana rock or a cheap Angel ([[Giada, Font of Hope]],
[[Starnheim Aspirant]]). Send back hands with only 7-drops.

**Early game (turns 1–3):** play a mana rock and a 2-drop Angel. [[Giada, Font of Hope]] on turn 2 is ideal,
because every Angel after it enters bigger. [[Bishop of Wings]] early gets lifegain going.

**Mid game (turns 4–6):** cast Lyra, then add a token payoff ([[Resplendent Angel]], [[Angelic Accord]],
[[Valkyrie Harbinger]]). Attack with everything that flies: one lifelink attack usually clears the 4–5 life
needed for a free 4/4. Keep a protection spell up if you can.

**Late game:** deploy the 7-drops. [[Serra's Emissary]] on "creature" usually ends things, and
[[Herald of Eternal Dawn]] keeps you alive while you finish. Hold [[Wrath of God]] or [[Sunblast Angel]] for
when an opponent's board is clearly ahead of yours.

**Common misplays:**
- Attacking with Lyra into open mana without protection. She's what makes the whole deck work.
- Forgetting the end-step token triggers check life gained **this turn**. Gain life on your own turn
  before the end step.
- Casting your own board wipe when you're ahead. Use [[Make a Stand]] or [[Unbreakable Formation]] to
  survive other people's wipes instead.

## Strengths & weaknesses

**Strengths:** huge life totals make you hard to race. Flyers get past most ground decks. The token payoffs
rebuild your board after spot removal. It's very cheap to put together.

**Weaknesses:**
- Board wipes hurt; only 4 protection pieces.
- The top end is heavy (several 7-drops).
- It has no instant-speed tricks against combo decks.
- Lifegain-hate cards like [[Erebos, God of the Dead]] or [[Tainted Remedy]] slow it down a lot.
