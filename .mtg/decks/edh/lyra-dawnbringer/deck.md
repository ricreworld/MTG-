## Actual bracket: **Bracket 2 (Core)**, same as the target

- **Game Changers: 0** (checked with `is:gamechanger`). Smothering Tithe, Teferi's Protection, Enlightened
  Tutor and Farewell were left out on purpose.
- No two-card infinite combos, no mass land destruction, no extra-turn cards, no tutors beyond basic-land
  searches. Sol Ring is the only fast mana.
- **To move up to Bracket 3:** add 1–3 Game Changers. In white the natural ones are Smothering Tithe (€39),
  Teferi's Protection (€29) and Enlightened Tutor (€17). Adding even one makes it Bracket 3. Restoring the
  reference cards (Archangel of Thune, Avacyn, Esper Sentinel) makes it stronger but keeps it Bracket 2.
- **What would push it higher than intended:** 4+ Game Changers, or an infinite like Heliod, Sun-Crowned +
  Walking Ballista. Avoid that pairing if you want to stay in Bracket 2–3.
